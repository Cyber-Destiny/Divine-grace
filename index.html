About us

Come in, settle down. We'll take it from here.

Divine Grace is a hotel, event centre and restaurant in one compound. Our suites have king-size beds, cold air-conditioning, shower cabins and ceiling lights you can switch to any colour you like.

Here for one night or a whole week? In town for a wedding at the hall? Or just after a quiet meal? Our staff are on duty day and night, so there's always someone to help.

  • Key-card doorsOnly your card opens your door, and there's a video intercom inside.
  • Secure compoundFenced and gated, with cameras in the corridors.
  • Event hallStage, lighting rig and an upper gallery for your guests.
  • Food & drinksA restaurant and two lounges, right downstairs.

Events

The Event Hall

A large, bright hall with a raised stage, an overhead lighting rig and a gallery level above the main floor. We host weddings, birthdays, conferences, church programmes and more.

  • Raised stage with a stone feature wall
  • Lighting rig with spotlights
  • Upper gallery overlooking the floor
  • Polished marble floor
  • Chairs and tables available
  • Air-conditioning

Restaurant

Come down for breakfast, grab a cold drink from the chiller or sit down for a proper meal in the evening. It's open to hotel guests, event guests and visitors.
