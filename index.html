Welcome

A house of quiet comfort that comes alive after dark.

At Divine Grace, every detail is chosen for how it makes you feel. Polished marble floors, tufted king-size beds and soft ceiling light that shifts from sapphire to ruby at the touch of a switch.

Stay the night, host a wedding in the Grand Hall, meet friends in the lounge or simply enjoy a quiet meal. Our uniformed team is here day and night to make it effortless.

Restaurant & Café

Good food, golden light.

Our restaurant and café is a calm, airy room wrapped in textured stone walls, with tall windows on one side and a warm, glowing ceiling cove under a crystal chandelier.

Drop in for breakfast before a meeting, a light lunch, chilled drinks from the cooler or a relaxed dinner. Hotel guests, event guests and visitors are all welcome.
